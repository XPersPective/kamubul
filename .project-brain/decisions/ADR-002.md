# ADR-002 — Secure Assistant Answer Reporting Contract (PB-037)
Status: ACCEPTED (25 October 2026)

## Context
Google Play and App Store generative AI policies require an in-app mechanism for users to report problematic AI-generated answers (`workers/src/assistant.js`, `lib/ui/assistant_chat.dart`). The reporting mechanism must preserve KamuBul's local-only privacy boundary (`C-003`), stay within Cloudflare Free D1/Worker quotas (`C-004`), prevent duplicate/spam submissions, and avoid uploading full chat history or personal profile data.

## Decision
1. **Minimal Payload & Privacy Boundary (`C-003`):**
   - A report submits only `{ installationId, reportId, listingId, revision, reason, questionExcerpt, answerExcerpt, note }` to `POST /v1/assistant/report`.
   - Full chat history and user profile/eligibility fields are never included; `questionExcerpt` ($\le 300$ chars), `answerExcerpt` ($\le 1200$ chars), and optional `note` ($\le 300$ chars) are previewed by the user before explicit confirmation.
2. **Idempotency & Rate Limiting (`C-004`):**
   - `reportId` is a client-generated deterministic UUID/hash per `(installationId, listingId, revision, answerHash)`. Repeated submissions of the same `reportId` return `200 OK` idempotently via `INSERT OR IGNORE INTO assistant_reports`.
   - Rate limited in D1 `rate_limits` to at most 5 reports per `installationId` per UTC day and 16 KB max request body.
3. **Local Draft Persistence & Backup Boundary:**
   - Unsent or failed report drafts stay in local SQLite (`ListingStore`) excluded from cloud auto-backup (`android:allowBackup="false"` / local app database) and are cleared once a `200 OK` receipt is stored.
4. **Retention, Deletion & Operator Access:**
   - Stored in D1 `assistant_reports` with a 30-day TTL (`expires_at`), pruned during the hourly `:59` maintenance cron in `workers/src/pipeline.js`.
   - Operator readback (`GET /v1/admin/assistant-reports`) and deletion (`DELETE /v1/admin/assistant-reports/:id`) require a constant-time verified bearer secret (`ADMIN_TOKEN` Worker secret) and never expose reports publicly.

## Consequences
`PB-037` has a complete trust, privacy, authorization, idempotency, retention, and deletion contract. Implementation in `workers/` and `lib/ui/assistant_chat.dart` must enforce these exact bounds and update `site/gizlilik/index.html` and `workers/public/privacy/` accordingly.
