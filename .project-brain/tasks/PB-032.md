## Objective
“Abonelik sistemi sorunsuz çalışıyor değil mi? ... geçiş reklam vesaire ... doğru müdahale ediyor mu?” Verify real product behavior and truthfully record any remaining gaps.

## Steps
1. Trace trial, subscription restore/expiry and ad gating through `lib/` and Android configuration; compare claims with actual behavior.
2. Identify the smallest missing automated checks for transitions and add them under `test/`.
3. Verify applicable Android flows in a stable QA environment and record which runtime/device checks remain open.

## Acceptance
- [ ] [machine] Tests cover trial-to-expired, Pro restore/expiry and ad visibility boundaries without exposing ads to active Pro users.
- [ ] [integration] Android QA readback confirms the applicable user flows; simulated tests are labeled as simulated.
- [ ] [interaction] Trial expiry, Pro restore/expiry, ad boundary and denied permission flows give clear results without false success.
- [ ] [visual] Phone/tablet, light/dark and enlarged text runtime settings remain readable without overflow.
- [ ] [accessibility] Keyboard and TalkBack can operate the applicable settings and subscription controls.
- [ ] [architecture] Product claims and unresolved runtime gaps are documented without assuming real billing or delivery from unit tests.
